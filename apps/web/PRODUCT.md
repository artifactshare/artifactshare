# Artifact Share

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People sharing work created by themselves, AI agents, or automation, and the people opening those shared links to read, watch, review, and comment.

## Product Purpose

Share artifacts at stable URLs so their creators and recipients can inspect the work without preparing a separate presentation or deployment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Share HTML, Markdown, folders, static sites, PDF documents, and MP4 videos.
- Control access through workspace and project audiences, specific people, or link sharing. Available actions depend on the caller's permissions and credential type.
- Replace shared content at a stable URL and support review through comments and version history.
- Keep application controls and artifact content in their existing rendering and security boundaries. Preserve uploaded HTML and the original typography inside PDFs.

## Product Principles

- Make the artifact the focus of the viewing experience.
- Provide useful, readable defaults before asking people to customize anything.
- Keep the interface simple and easy to operate.
- Preserve familiar behavior while making shared actions consistent across formats.
- Use the existing design system and verify changes on desktop and mobile.

## Brand Commitments

Artifact Share's existing name, brand assets, interface vocabulary, and component system are the incumbent identity. Refinements extend that identity.
