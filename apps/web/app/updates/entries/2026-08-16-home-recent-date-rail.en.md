date: 2026-08-16

Recently seen on Home now places dates in a compact rail beside each row. You can keep the viewing date as a memory cue while seeing more files at once.

Long titles are shortened to one line, and files viewed on the same day stay aligned without repeating the date. The full Recently seen page keeps its existing date headings.
