---
title: Home splits into My files and Recently seen tabs
date: 2026-07-24
products: [web]
kind: new
notice: true
---

Home now has two tabs, My files and Recently seen. Recently seen lists your own uploads together with other people's files you opened through a share link, ordered by when you last viewed each one, and leaves out anything you no longer have access to. You can search the list by file name, title, project, or owner, and page through 20 items at a time to reach your full history. This makes it easier to get back to a file you opened once through a share link, including ones shared deep in a chat or message thread.
