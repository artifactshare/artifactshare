title: Manage active CLI sessions
date: 2026-08-11
products: [web, cli, admin]

---

Settings → Tokens now lists active Artifact Share CLI login sessions. You can revoke one session or revoke them all at once.

<!-- more -->

Workspace owners and admins can also revoke a member's CLI sessions. Removing a member from a workspace now revokes their CLI sessions too.
