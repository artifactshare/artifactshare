---
title: See who viewed your files
date: 2026-08-19
products: [web]
kind: new
notice: true
---

On the shared page you can now open "Who viewed" to see who in your workspace opened a file and when. Open it from the viewer count next to the view counter, or from "Who viewed" in the "…" menu.

- The list is visible only to people in the same workspace who can view that file. Viewing from outside your workspace is not included.
- Viewing history from before this feature became available is also shown.
- Entries record that a file was opened; they are not read receipts.
