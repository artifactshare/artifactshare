Artifact Share now uses Google Analytics 4 to see how people move from viewing a shared file, through signing in and creating a workspace, to posting a first file. This helps us find where visitors drop off. The privacy policy has been updated to describe this in full.

<!-- more -->

Visitors in the EU, the EEA, and the UK are asked for consent before analytics starts; elsewhere it runs by default and can be turned off anytime from "Analytics settings" in the page footer or a shared page. No email address, name, company name, file contents, or credentials are sent to Google — signed-in activity is connected using a pseudonymous identifier that does not directly identify you.

First posts made through the CLI or MCP are also measured, using a server-to-server signal rather than the browser.
