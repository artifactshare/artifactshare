title: Workspace settings redesigned
date: 2026-07-17
products: [web]
kind: improve
---

The workspace settings screens have been redesigned. Wider layouts let tables display without horizontal scrolling, and a new General page brings together workspace name editing and role permissions.

<!-- more -->

Settings pages used to have a narrow layout, so tables like the member list and the inventory view didn't fit and required horizontal scrolling. On screens 1280px wide or larger, that scrolling is no longer needed.

Changing the workspace name and understanding what owners, admins, and members can each do used to live on separate pages. Both are now on a single new General page.

Settings navigation is now consistently tab-based. On the inventory page, the Projects and Files tabs each have their own URL, so you can share a link to a specific tab.

The billing page is now organized into three sections: current plan, this month's estimated charges, and storage usage. Usage and inventory lists now paginate, so large lists stay responsive. Keyboard focus indicators are also now visible throughout the settings screens.
