---
title: Transfer a removed member's files and restore members
date: 2026-07-13
products: [admin]
kind: new
---

Workspace admins can now complete offboarding entirely from the settings page.

<!-- more -->

When removing a member, you can choose who takes over the files they own. This includes items in their personal inbox and privately shared files, which the new owner can then view and manage. Authorship stays with the original creator.

You can also transfer files later from the "Removed members" section in settings. If someone was removed by mistake or rejoins the company, you can restore them from the same place.
