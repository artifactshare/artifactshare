Public pricing pages are now available in Japanese and English. Workspace owners can choose Plus or Team, monthly or yearly billing, and JPY or USD from billing settings, then continue to Checkout.

Plan prices do not vary by contributor count. Plus includes 10 GB of storage, and Team includes 100 GB. Active subscriptions can continue beyond the included storage for ¥16 per GB-month or $0.10 per GB-month.
